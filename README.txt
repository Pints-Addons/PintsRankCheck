Pint's Rank Check

Learned a new rank of Frostbolt or Healing Wave? The old rank stays on your action bars until you drag the new one up
yourself. Rank Check notices: whenever you learn spells (and when you log in) it looks over every action-bar button,
and if any hold a lower rank than the best one you know, a small window lists them:

  Frostbolt  Rank 4 -> Rank 5      Bar 1, button 3      [Swap]  [ ] Keep

Replace all swaps every one at once; Swap does just that button. Tick Keep on a button you leave on a low rank on
purpose (a cheap heal, say) and it's left alone from then on.

/ranks - check now     /ranks reset - forget every Keep

Action bars can't be changed in combat - if you learn a rank mid-fight, the window waits until the fight is over.
Works on WoW Classic Era and WoW Forever. Macros aren't touched.
